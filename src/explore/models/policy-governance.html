Regulatory change is not the same as reduced burden. Institutional reform matters when people can access fairer, faster and more dependable services.

The IP3 governance cycle

Five decisions.
One delivery mandate.

No reform should begin and end with a decree. Effective governance follows a testable sequence from bottleneck, to agreement, to service redesign, to implementation, to citizen-level verification.

What improved?

Audit time, cost, access, compliance, uptake and unintended harms.
