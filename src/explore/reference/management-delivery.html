      Transparent methodology: 120 identical notional work packages; productivity = 0.75 packages per team member per week at 100% availability. Effective weekly throughput = members × 0.75 × availability × (1 − dependency drag) ÷ (1 + rework). Forecast finish = 120 ÷ throughput, rounded up to full weeks. Baseline is fixed at 10 people, 65% availability, 24% rework, and 23% dependency drag. Target = 24 weeks. Progress lines assume constant throughput and no learning curve. The model excludes critical-path constraints, procurement delays, budget limits, team ramp-up and task heterogeneity. It is a synthetic teaching illustration and must not be presented as observed IP3 client outcomes or a validated programme forecast.
