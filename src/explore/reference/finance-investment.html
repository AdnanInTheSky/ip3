Finance as a decision discipline.
Public value is not the same thing as cash flow. A project can be economically justified yet financially unsustainable without an operating and financing plan.
What every investment must answer

Five tests before the decision.

A rigorous investment case integrates welfare effects, commercial economics, affordability, exposure to risk, and the operating model. Each filter closes a different blind spot.

For public investments, the model illustrates economic value, not fiscal cash availability. O&M funding and financial viability need separate analysis.

Calculation: −initial investment + discounted annual (gross benefit × [1 − stress] − recurring cost × [1 + stress ÷ 2]). One equal annual flow is assumed for each year of the illustrative horizon. IRR is solved from the same stream. This is a teaching model, not a project forecast, appraisal, financing offer, or estimate of actual client outcomes.
