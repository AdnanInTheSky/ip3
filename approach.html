Reform is a movement, not a moment.

Complex challenges rarely fail for want of another recommendation. They fail when evidence is disconnected from decisions, strategy from financing, technology from users, and reform from the institutions that must own it.

From concept start to delivery state

We begin with the problem as it exists — not with a pre-packaged solution.

Every transformation starts somewhere different. A ministry may have strong policy but weak implementation capacity. A programme may have funding but no unclear delivery model. An institution system may have trusted relationships but no data culture. A city may have the infrastructure it needs but an inaccessible business model. A public institution may generate large volumes of data and still struggle to turn them into decisions.

THE PATHWAY

Six Movements of Reform

From diagnosis to durable institutional capability. The movements are shown in order because a roadmap must be understandable — but evidence can change the design, a plan can invalidate an assumption, and implementation can expose an institutional constraint. Feedback and adaptation are built in from the start.

01

Sense & Diagnose

Understand the system before moving to improve. A credible problem is rarely the whole problem. We run a current-state diagnostic that identifies how policies, institutions, investments, markets, people, technology, finance, infrastructure, data and behaviours interact — separating what drives the problem from what is merely a symptom, and locating where intervention creates the greatest leverage.

Case Study Example — Ghana Subsoil Fertiliser: Ghana's soil health crisis wasn't about fertiliser availability — it was about the entire system: subsidy design, distribution channels, farmer trust, soil testing, data flows, and last-mile logistics. IP3 mapped the full value chain to identify where intervention would create the greatest leverage.
  • Client QuestionWhat is really preventing the organisation, programme, market or citizen from reaching the intended outcome?
  • What We DeliverCurrent-State Diagnostic & Transformation Readiness
  • Deliverable Map & MatrixInstitutional evidence, process and digital maturity diagnostic.
02

Build Intelligence

Turn evidence into decisions, not just reports. Not every evidence gap requires another large study. We identify what must be known to change a decision, combine existing evidence with new primary research where it is needed, and connect quantitative analysis to institutional, behavioural and user insight. Evidence is then translated into a practical target state.

Case Study Example — Technology Adoption in Low-Land Holdings: For a multi-country initiative, research on farm design and tool improvements, agronomics and financing, market, user and gender dynamics, extension models, and behaviour change was woven into a single, actionable strategy.
  • Client QuestionWhat do we need to know to act, and differently?
  • What We DeliverEvidence & Target State Blueprint
  • Deliverable Map & MatrixDecision-grade data and service analytics linked to a practical target state.
03

Co-Design & Test

Turn insight into a credible, testable system. This is where analysis becomes actionable architecture. Policies, programmes, operating models, digital systems, regulations, financial mechanisms and services are designed together with the institutions and people who must use them — then prototyped and tested before anyone commits institutional resources.

Case Study Example — Malawi Farmer Transformation & Digital Lending: IP3 worked with Malawi's government, financial sector, and NGOs to co-design a farmer transformation and digital lending system. The design integrated land tenure, credit scoring, extension services, market access, and mobile money — tested with real farmers and financial institutions before national rollout.
  • Client QuestionWhat solution can we validate with real institutions, users, finance and political constraints?
  • What We DeliverTarget Solution Architecture
  • Deliverable Map & MatrixTarget operating model, service blueprint and data architecture, prototyped.
04

Mobilise

A good design means nothing if it isn't resourced. Mobilisation is broader than financing. It means aligning the mandate, leadership, stakeholders, people, capabilities, technology, data, financing and implementation resources needed to move from design into operating reality — and getting early, client-facing proof of programme and leadership teams rather than handed to a downstream consultant.

Case Study Example — Ghana Subsoil Early Warning System: IP3 designed a decision-support tool for the Ministry of Agriculture. We mapped stakeholder roles, financing pathways, data governance, and change management — ensuring the system could be owned and sustained by Ghanaian institutions, not just delivered as a technical product.
  • Client QuestionWhat must be aligned, funded, and resourced to move from design to implementation in practice?
  • What We DeliverMobilisation & Implementation Readiness Plan
  • Deliverable Map & MatrixMandate alignment, stakeholder mapping and financing plan.
05

Implement & Transfer

Implementation is part of consulting — not what happens after consulting. We work alongside clients as strategy becomes operating reality, tracking change management and stakeholder engagement as implementation disciplines rather than communication exercises. The objective is not consultant dependency — it is institutional capability, transferred deliberately as the work proceeds.

Case Study Example — Municipal Finance & Institutional Transformation: IP3 supported a municipal finance transformation, embedding change management, stakeholder engagement, and capacity transfer into every phase. The result was a system owned and operated by local institutions, not dependent on external consultants.
  • Client QuestionHow do we build our own policy programme, service or system and sustain it ourselves?
  • What We DeliverOperational Delivery System & Capability Transfer Plan
  • Deliverable Map & MatrixProcess redesign, partner rollout and skills transfer capability tracking.
06

Learn & Scale

Measure to drive change, not justify it. Transformation evaluation asks what happened after an intervention ends. Our M&E & Approach asks what the decision-makers need to know while implementation is still underway — so course corrections can be made and lessons applied before implementation closes, so evidence can change the programme rather than merely report on it.

Case Study Example — Policy Reform, Trade & LDC Graduation: IP3 supported a country's transition from LDC status, designing M&E frameworks that tracked trade, investment, and institutional capacity in real time — enabling course corrections before graduation deadlines.
  • Client QuestionWhat evidence would tell us whether to adapt, stop, institutionalise or scale?
  • What We DeliverM&E & Adaptation & Scale Architecture
  • Deliverable Map & MatrixLive dashboards and performance triggers linking feedback and scale.
The standard inside every phase

Six commitments that make the pathway IP3.

The pathway describes the sequence. These describe the standard we hold ourselves to inside it.

01

Evidence Before Assumption

Decisions are only as good as the evidence they rest on. We do not start with solutions. We start with the problem as it exists, and let the evidence determine the pathway.

02

System Before Symptoms

Every policy problem is part of a larger system. We address root causes, not symptoms. Connected problems require connected solutions. We map the full system — institutions, markets, technology, users and behaviours — before defining the intervention.

03

Co-Creation, Not Prescription

A solution imposed is a solution resisted. We design with government, markets, communities and users. The best solutions emerge from the people who must own and sustain them.

04

Test Before Scale

Designing at scale is designing blind. We prototype, test, and iterate. The pathway is built on evidence, not assumption. What works in pilot informs what works at scale.

05

Implementation Alongside Strategy

Policy, research, finance, digital design, and delivery are not separate phases. They are one continuous process. We design with the end in mind, and stay with the client through implementation and adaptation.

06

Capability Beyond the Engagement

Our job is to make ourselves unnecessary. The ultimate measure of success is not the report we deliver, but the capability we leave behind. We build institutional capacity so clients can sustain change after the engagement.

Design with the people who live the system

Policy becomes real through people’s experience.

We combine what the data measures with what people experience. Even the most well-designed policies fail when they don't account for the lived experience of the people who must implement and use them. Technical correctness alone is insufficient without human-centered service design connecting people, process, data, tech, and finance.
