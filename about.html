Who we are

Built for complex systems. Anchored in local realities.

The Institute for Public Policy and Practice (IP3) is a next-generation think tank and policy advisory firm founded in 2018 to transform how governments, multilaterals and communities in the Global South tackle today’s most urgent challenges. Unlike legacy consultancies bound by siloed expertise and static methods, IP3 combines systems thinking, data and digital ecosystems, and translational research to deliver adaptive, resilient solutions across the entire policy advisory value chain.

As a translational policy studio, IP3 bridges visionary ideas and grounded execution. We accelerate policy design, co-implementation and continuous learning through cross-sectoral innovation and locally anchored practice — turning evidence and insight into measurable impact.

Complex public challenges rarely fit within a single discipline. IP3 brings together economists, policy specialists, researchers, sector experts, data strategists and practitioners from diverse geographies — with a particular focus on the operational realities of the Global South.

Our vision

Policy innovation for transformative, sustainable growth

We envision a future where governments, development partners, businesses and communities have the policies, institutions, data and capabilities to navigate complexity and create inclusive, resilient and sustainable growth. Policy innovation is more than designing better policies: it is building the systems that turn ambition into action — connecting evidence, institutional capacity, digital innovation, stakeholder intelligence and implementation.

Our mission

Turning evidence, innovation and implementation into lasting impact

We help governments, multilaterals and development partners, private enterprises and communities solve overlapping, cascading and compounding policy and management challenges through translational research, strategic policy advisory, systems thinking, data-driven decision-making, action research and implementation support.

  • Strengthen resilient and effective institutions
  • Accelerate sustainable and circular economic transitions
  • Improve education systems and human capabilities
  • Enable data-driven and digitally empowered governance
  • Advance inclusive and equitable service delivery
  • Translate policy ambition into scalable, locally relevant solutions
Founding principles

Poly-crises demand poly-solutions.

In an era where AI intersects with climate, ESG overlaps biodiversity, and informal economies mesh with digital governance, single-axis approaches fail. IP3 was born to fill that gap: a practice-driven, cross-disciplinary platform built for the complexity of now — and the transformation of what’s next.
