How might simplification, digital uptake and rework change administrative effort?

Method
The baseline is 1.5 core hours plus 24% rework at 2.2 hours per instance. Up to five of ten assumed steps can be removed. Digital uptake and simplification affect core time; rework is modelled separately.
Key assumption
Baseline: 1.5 core hours + 0.24 × 2.2 rework hours per case. Digital and step coefficients, monthly volume and BDT 650/hour are synthetic.
Reading the threshold
Zero annual hours released means no workload change. Negative values mean extra workload. Staff-time opportunity value does not establish a budget saving or fiscal space.
Evidence needed before a real decision

Collect time-and-motion observations, demand volumes, channel uptake, rework reasons, implementation costs and service-quality/distributional measures.

Released capacity requires an operational plan; service quality, access, transition costs and actual cash expenditure are outside the model.

From public policy to public value

Make every rule
work for people.

Better policy is not an isolated memo or an automated workflow. It is a credible agreement about who must do what, how resources and authority move, what people experience, and how the results will be checked. IP3 brings economic reasoning, institutional research and practical delivery design to that accountability challenge.

Interpretation & methodology

Understand the assumptions.

The baseline is 1.5 core hours plus 24% rework at 2.2 hours per instance. Up to five of ten assumed steps can be removed. Digital uptake and simplification affect core time; rework is modelled separately.

Calculation and units

Baseline = 1.5 + 0.24 × 2.2 hours; new core = 1.5 × (1−0.065 × steps) × (1−0.36 × digital uptake); new total = core + rework × 2.2; annual hours released = (baseline−new total) × monthly cases × 12.

In formulas, tariff, FX change, discount rate, stress, productivity, digital uptake, rework, availability and dependency drag use fractions where applicable (8% = 0.08). Slider labels show percentages. Elasticity is unitless; outcome differences use percentage points.

What this model cannot establish

All coefficients and the BDT 650/hour valuation are synthetic. Time opportunity value is not a cash saving or budget reduction. Negative released hours mean workload increased.

There is no empirical calibration dataset in the handoff. Changing a scenario changes its narrative; controls are a shared teaching model, except Finance, where each case also defines its own cash-flow assumptions.
