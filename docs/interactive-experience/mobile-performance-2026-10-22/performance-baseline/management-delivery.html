From strategy on paper to results in practice

Make the complex
deliverable.

IP3 brings economic reasoning and hands-on programme management together: developing realistic workplans, clarifying institutional ownership, coordinating specialists, managing evidence and building systems that make implementation observable. The measure of good management is not how much was planned, but what can be delivered, sustained and learned.

Interpretation & methodology

Understand the assumptions.

A linear throughput illustration with 120 equivalent work packages, 0.75 packages/person/week and an illustrative 24-week target. The fixed comparison uses 10 people, 65% availability, 24% rework and 23% dependency drag.

Calculation and units

Weekly rate = team × 0.75 × availability × (1−dependency drag)/(1+rework); duration = ceil(120/rate); schedule gap = 24−duration.

In formulas, tariff, FX change, discount rate, stress, productivity, digital uptake, rework, availability and dependency drag use fractions where applicable (8% = 0.08). Slider labels show percentages. Elasticity is unitless; outcome differences use percentage points.

What this model cannot establish

This is not a critical-path or resource-loaded schedule. It has no real dependencies, holidays, cost model or uncertainty distribution. The chart horizon expands to include slow scenarios.

There is no empirical calibration dataset in the handoff. Changing a scenario changes its narrative; controls are a shared teaching model, except Finance, where each case also defines its own cash-flow assumptions.
