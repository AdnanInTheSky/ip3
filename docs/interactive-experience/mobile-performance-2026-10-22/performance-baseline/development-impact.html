How does the treated group’s before–after change compare with the comparison group’s change?

Method
Two groups observed before and after an intervention. The arithmetic compares their changes, measured in percentage points (pp). Causal interpretation requires credible identification, including parallel trends.
Key assumption
Four synthetic outcome percentages and one constructed counterfactual. There is no sample, standard error or confidence interval.
Reading the threshold
A zero DiD contrast means equal changes in this illustration. A nonzero contrast alone establishes no causal effect. Counterfactuals outside 0–100% flag infeasible percentage assumptions.
Evidence needed before a real decision

Check pre-intervention trends, group composition, treatment timing, spillovers, concurrent shocks, attrition and missingness; then specify an identification and inference strategy.

From research to better development

Make every result
account for something.

Good evaluation does more than report success. It tells governments, donors and businesses when to invest, what to redesign, who benefits, who is excluded and which outcomes are worth scaling. IP3 connects rigorous inquiry with practical decisions—and acknowledges uncertainty where the evidence ends.

Interpretation & methodology

Understand the assumptions.

Two groups observed before and after an intervention. The arithmetic compares their changes, measured in percentage points (pp). Causal interpretation requires credible identification, including parallel trends.

Calculation and units

Treated change = treated endline − baseline; comparison change = comparison endline − baseline; DiD = treated change − comparison change; counterfactual = treated baseline + comparison change.

In formulas, tariff, FX change, discount rate, stress, productivity, digital uptake, rework, availability and dependency drag use fractions where applicable (8% = 0.08). Slider labels show percentages. Elasticity is unitless; outcome differences use percentage points.

What this model cannot establish

There are no observations, uncertainty intervals or parallel-trend diagnostics. Arithmetic alone establishes no causal effect. Extreme inputs can produce an infeasible counterfactual outside 0–100%; the chart displays and flags that result without silently clipping it.

There is no empirical calibration dataset in the handoff. Changing a scenario changes its narrative; controls are a shared teaching model, except Finance, where each case also defines its own cash-flow assumptions.

Does a learning product improve capability—not just uptake?

A training or education technology provider may count enrolments and active accounts while remaining uncertain whether students or workers actually improve their competencies.

Decision challenge

  • Customer outcomes are poorly measured

Illustrative decision outputs

  • Compare learner progress against a credible benchmark

Evidence and diagnosis

  • Usage & completion analytics
  • Outcome dashboards
