# Template text inventory

Scope: all user-facing literals and dynamic text slots in `src/pages-home.js`, `src/pages-inner.js`, `src/pages-why.js` and `src/layout.js`. Marketing copy is supplied by the CMS. The remaining literals are recorded below by role so accessibility and UI labels are not mistaken for marketing copy.

## CMS-managed marketing text

| Content form | Fields managed there |
|---|---|
| `content/pages.json` | Browser/search titles and descriptions; page hero headline and lead for About, Why choose IP3, Approach, Capabilities, Sectors, Engagements, Insights, People and Contact. |
| `content/page-text/home.json` | Home section headings/eyebrows/leads and marketing CTAs: capabilities lead; explore/all-capabilities, all-engagements, all-insights, all-sectors, commission-dialogue, meet-people and start-conversation buttons; sector intro; leadership eyebrow. |
| `content/page-text/about.json` | About section headings/eyebrows/leads; supporting paragraph; people/conversation CTAs; comparison labels. |
| `content/page-text/why.json` | Why choose IP3 section headings (capabilities, sectors, selected work, method, closing CTA), hero shortcut line, scroll cue, 3D on/off labels, sector-map labels, terminal status label and button labels. |
| `content/why.json` | The /why-ip3 3D journey: opening audience line and buttons, the six scenes, systems and their links, evidence layers, diagram titles and captions, policy and practice steps, 3D building labels, impact outcomes, the giant bridge word, the brand film and the Policy Intelligence Terminal walkthrough. |
| `content/page-text/approach.json` | Approach section headings/eyebrows and the Define Decision, Why Different and Beyond Recommendation labels. |
| `content/page-text/capabilities.json` | Capabilities section headings and CTAs. |
| `content/page-text/sectors.json` | Sector headings/eyebrows, challenge and introduction copy, Discuss Assignment and All Sectors CTAs. |
| `content/page-text/engagements.json` | Engagements headings and CTAs. |
| `content/page-text/insights.json` | Insights headings and CTAs; publications heading, filter labels, note and new-tab notice. |
| `content/page-text/people.json` | People headings, founder eyebrow and Talk CTA; profile open/close, practice and education labels. |
| `content/page-text/contact.json` | Contact headings, map CTA, form submit/status/privacy copy. |
| `content/page-text/notfound.json` | 404 heading, lead and the Back Home, Capabilities and Contact buttons. |
| `content/navigation.json` | Header and drawer navigation, CTA, breadcrumbs, footer link lists, footer labels, sector navigation labels and contact pathways. |
| `content/legal.json` | Privacy and Terms titles, leads, update date, section headings and paragraph copy. |

The ten numbered Page text forms are generated by `cms/schema.js` and placed first in the Pages CMS sidebar. Heading titles permit `<em>` only for the 30 template keys that already contained emphasis; validation rejects new emphasis on the other heading keys. Other content continues to come from its existing schema-backed JSON forms.
