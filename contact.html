Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

What types of engagements does IP3 undertake?

Diagnostics, policy and program design, project preparation, implementation support, evaluations, convening processes and research partnerships. We work as prime consultant or subconsultant depending on the mandate.

Does IP3 work as prime consultant or subconsultant?

Both. We lead assignments where our capabilities are the core requirement, and join larger multi-firm engagements as a specialist partner where our sector or technical expertise adds specific value.

How do you approach quality assurance and fiduciary requirements?

Deliverables undergo internal peer review and senior technical review. We design our work to meet the fiduciary and safeguard expectations of multilateral and bilateral development partners.

Can IP3 provide verified references?

Yes. References from previous clients are available on request, subject to confidentiality agreements where applicable.

How quickly can IP3 mobilize a team?

This depends on scope and specialist requirements. Tell us the timeline and we will confirm a realistic mobilization plan when we respond.

How does IP3 handle confidentiality and NDAs?

We routinely work under NDA and can execute client-specific confidentiality agreements before a proposal is submitted.

What happens after I submit a message or consultation request?

Our team reviews every submission. For general messages you will receive an acknowledgment and response within one business day.

Where is IP3 located for in-person consultations?

At our Dhaka office: Zenith Prime, House-39, Road-35/A, Gulshan-2, Dhaka-1212.
