How an engagement starts

Every engagement can begin at any stage.

01

Diagnose

Map systems, institutions, incentives, data ecosystems, risks and underlying policy constraints.

02

Co-design

Combine evidence, stakeholder knowledge and action research to develop practical policies, programs, regulatory frameworks and institutional solutions.

03

Test & learn

Pilot interventions, experiment with delivery models, generate implementation evidence and adapt solutions to what works in practice.

04

Implement & scale

Support institutional adoption, capacity development, digital and data systems, implementation and adaptive MERLA to strengthen performance over time.
